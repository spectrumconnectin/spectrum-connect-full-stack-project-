## Design System

### Brand Colors
- **Primary**: White (#FFFFFF)
- **Accent**: Cobalt Blue (#195AD7)
- **Secondary Accent**: Teal/Emerald (#10B981)
- **Neutrals**: Gray-50 (#F8FAFC), Gray-100 (#F1F5F9), Gray-900 (#111827)
- **Semantic**: Red (#EF4444) for urgency, Amber (#F59E0B) for attention

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Inter Bold, 48-56px (titles), 32-40px (subtitles)
- **Body**: Inter Regular, 18-20px (main copy), 14-16px (supporting)
- **Captions**: Inter Medium, 12-14px
- **Line height**: 1.4 for headlines, 1.6 for body

### Layout Grid
- 16-column grid
- 60px margins (desktop), 30px (mobile)
- 24px gutter
- Max-width: 1280px

### Visual Style
- Minimal, clean lines
- Generous whitespace (40-50% empty space)
- Subtle gradients (max 2 colors, 15-20° angle)
- Modern photography (hero images: people, creative work, tech)
- Custom illustrations with 3-4 color palette
- 12px border radius on UI elements
- Consistent icon style: Feather or custom 2px stroke icons

- Each level shows market size and calculation basis
- Supporting annotations and metrics

### Market Breakdown

#### TAM (Total Addressable Market)
- **Size**: $10.2 Billion
- **Calculation**: 
  - Creator economy: $104B x 9.8% (marketplace penetration)
  - Freelance platforms: $14.3B (60% creator-side)
- **Explanation**: "All creators globally seeking direct client relationships"

#### SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)
- **Size**: $2.4 Billion
- **Calculation**:
  - Focus: Video, Design, Photography, Music, Writing
  - Geographic: North America, EU, APAC (tier 1)
  - Assumption: 23% of TAM (focused verticals + regions)
- **Explanation**: "Creators in high-value creative fields, developed markets"

#### SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
- **Size**: $156 Million (Year 5)
- **Calculation**:
  - 3.2M creators on platform
  - $48.75 average annual value per creator
  - Assumption: 10% platform adoption in SAM
- **Explanation**: "Realistic 5-year capture with disciplined go-to-market"

### Visual Recommendations
- Pyramid diagram (three layers):
  - Base (TAM): Widest, light blue
  - Middle (SAM): Medium, cobalt
  - Top (SOM): Narrowest, teal
- Each layer labeled with size, color-coded
- Annotations on the side with supporting metrics
- Icons: Globe (TAM), Target (SAM), Crown (SOM)

### Alternative: Venn Diagram
- Three overlapping circles:
  - Creators seeking direct work

### Design Notes

## Tone & Messaging Guidelines

**For All Slides:**
- **Confident without arrogance**: Show conviction but respect competition
- **Creator-centric language**: Everything through the lens of creator value
- **Data-backed**: Every claim should have evidence or be clearly marked as projection
- **Visual over textual**: Let charts and mockups carry the message
- **Story arc**: Build from problem → opportunity → solution → business → vision
- **Investor-friendly**: Numbers, timelines, and exit clarity matter

**Avoid:**
- Hype language without substance ("revolutionary," "disrupting," etc.)
- Vague metrics ("millions of users" without specifics)
- Over-promising (be conservative on projections)
- Jargon without explanation
- Cluttered slides (max 3-4 key points per slide)
